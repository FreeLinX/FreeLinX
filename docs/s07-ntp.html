Step 7: setup-ntp

Two choices: ntpd, or nothing. Re-running replaces the old answer instead of stacking — the previous link is removed first either way.

Enabling is one symlink. Definitions live in /etc/svc, runit runs what is under /var/service:

ln -sfn /etc/svc/ntpd /var/service/ntpd

ntpd is also on in the image before you choose. The clock is usually wrong on first boot, which fails every certificate check until it is roughly right, so keeping time is the default and "nothing" warns you about exactly that.
